# OIA — Complete Question Reference

> All 36 audit questions with IDs, weights, polarity, pillar, and dimension assignments.

---

## Quick Reference

- **36 Yes/No questions** across 4 pillars (9 each)
- **Weight pattern per triplet:** 5 (Critical) → 3 (Standard) → 1 (Indicator)
- **Polarity:** 1st question in every triplet is NEGATIVE (Yes = 0), 2nd and 3rd are POSITIVE (Yes = weight)
- **Max per pillar:** 27 points | **Max total:** 108 points

---

## DIAGNOSE — Clarity & Flow (q1–q9)

**What this pillar measures:** Whether the business has clear decision-making structures, visible workflows, and well-defined roles. This is the foundation — without clarity, nothing downstream works reliably.

### Decision Clarity (q1–q3)

| ID | W | Polarity | Question |
|----|---|----------|----------|
| q1 | 5 | NEG | Do decisions frequently stall because your team isn't clear on how you want choices made? |
| q2 | 3 | POS | Do you have a clear structure for who makes which decisions across your operations? |
| q3 | 1 | POS | Do you consistently communicate how decisions should flow so people don't rely on guesswork? |

### Workflow Awareness (q4–q6)

| ID | W | Polarity | Question |
|----|---|----------|----------|
| q4 | 5 | NEG | Do tasks slow down because key steps in your workflow live in people's heads instead of in a system? |
| q5 | 3 | POS | Does your team clearly understand how work moves from start to finish? |
| q6 | 1 | POS | Do you regularly review workflows to catch breakdowns before they cause drag? |

### Role Understanding (q7–q9)

| ID | W | Polarity | Question |
|----|---|----------|----------|
| q7 | 5 | NEG | Do tasks bounce back to you because roles aren't defined tightly enough? |
| q8 | 3 | POS | Does your team know exactly what "success" looks like in their role? |
